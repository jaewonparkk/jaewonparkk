## Featured projects

### 🦅 [EagleCurrent](https://github.com/jaewonparkk/eaglecurrent-platform)

**🏆 1st Place — 2026 Eagles Sustainability Competition**

A full-stack campus discovery platform that helps Boston College students find relevant events, explore 400+ organizations, and navigate activities through personalized recommendations and a live campus map.

`TypeScript` `React` `Supabase` `Maps` `Recommendation Systems`

### 🤖 [Blocks & Bots Assistant](https://github.com/jaewonparkk/scratchjr-chatbot)

A curriculum-grounded AI assistant for educators teaching ScratchJr, micro:bit, and classroom robotics—with dedicated educator, researcher, and administrator workspaces.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Gemini` `Supabase` `RAG` `pgvector`

### 🏨 [Hotel Ops Optimizer](https://github.com/jaewonparkk/hotel-ops-optimizer)

An end-to-end housekeeping optimization platform that uses the Hungarian algorithm and compares its results against a greedy baseline.

`Python` `FastAPI` `React` `Optimization` `Algorithms`

### 🎯 [FocusDock](https://github.com/jaewonparkk/focusdock)

An offline-first IoT productivity device combining an ESP32 touchscreen timer, cloud infrastructure, and a real-time focus analytics dashboard.

`C++` `ESP32` `IoT` `WebSockets` `Offline-first`
